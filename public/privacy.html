Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

In brief: Dailume is designed to keep planner content on the device where you create it. The current native iOS app and website do not use an account, analytics, advertising, or a Dailume-operated server. Website entries are stored in that browser's local storage; native entries are stored on the device. They do not sync with each other in this version.

Information we handle

  • Planner content: titles, details, dates, time blocks, completion status, and categories that you enter. The website stores this in browser local storage. The native iOS app stores this locally on your device.
  • Notifications: if you enable notifications in the iOS app, iOS schedules local event reminders on that device. Dailume does not send this content to a notification server.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages delivers the website. GitHub may process standard technical request data under its own policies when you visit the site.

What we do not do

The current version does not create a Dailume account, transmit planner content to a Dailume server, use Supabase, sell personal information, display ads, or include analytics SDKs. We cannot promise that data remains available if you clear browser storage, delete the app, reset your device, or lose the device.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete individual items inside the app. To clear website data, use your browser's site-data controls for this website. To remove native data, delete the relevant entries in the app or uninstall the app; uninstalling may remove its locally stored data. Keep your own backup of anything important.

Children and sensitive information

Dailume is a general-purpose planner, not a secure vault. Do not store passwords, payment-card details, medical records, or other highly sensitive information in it.

Changes

If the app later adds iCloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before those features are released.

Contact and support

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in the Dailume GitHub repository. Please do not include private planner content or sensitive personal information in public issues.
